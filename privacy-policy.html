Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

ByaheGas PH ("ByaheGas," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the ByaheGas PH website and how that information may be used.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About ByaheGas PH

ByaheGas PH is a free Philippine road trip fuel estimation website that allows users to estimate fuel requirements and potential fuel costs based on information such as route, vehicle, fuel type, fuel efficiency, fuel prices, and trip type.

2. Information You Provide

ByaheGas PH does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the fuel estimator.

Information entered into the calculator may include:

  • Starting point
  • Destination
  • Vehicle make, model, or variant
  • Fuel type
  • Fuel efficiency
  • Fuel price
  • Trip type
  • Fuel allowance

This information is used to perform the requested fuel estimation. ByaheGas PH does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the calculator.

3. Location Information

The website allows you to search for starting points and destinations. Location searches may be sent to third-party geocoding and mapping services in order to identify locations and calculate road distances.

ByaheGas PH does not intentionally store your submitted starting point or destination as a personal profile or account record.

Please avoid entering private or sensitive information into location fields.

4. Third-Party Services

ByaheGas PH may use third-party services to provide certain website functions. These services may process information necessary to provide their respective services.

These may include:

  • OpenRouteService for geocoding and road-distance calculations.
  • Photon / Komoot for location suggestions and geocoding.
  • Google Sheets and Google Apps Script for fuel-price and vehicle database information.
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting.
  • Google services, including Google AdSense or Google Analytics, if these services are enabled on the website.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. ByaheGas PH does not control the privacy practices of external services.

5. Fuel Price Information

Fuel prices displayed by ByaheGas PH may be obtained from a maintained fuel-price database and may represent average, estimated, or reference prices.

Fuel prices can vary between fuel companies, stations, locations, and dates. Prices displayed on the website should not be considered a guaranteed price at any particular fuel station.

6. Vehicle Information

Vehicle fuel-efficiency information may be based on reference data, manufacturer specifications, published information, estimates, or database values.

Actual fuel consumption may differ depending on vehicle condition, driving behavior, traffic, road conditions, passenger and cargo weight, weather, maintenance, tire pressure, and other factors.

ByaheGas PH itself may use minimal browser technologies necessary for website functionality.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about website usage.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable settings.

If advertising is implemented, additional information regarding advertising cookies and consent mechanisms may be provided through the website.

9. Analytics

ByaheGas PH may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website, including information such as pages visited, general traffic patterns, device information, and referring websites.

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies.

10. How Information Is Used
